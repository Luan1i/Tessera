What we heard

The brief

A small, free tiling window manager for Windows 11 that keeps every window in a clean grid, never tiles ghost windows, and lets you rearrange tiles by dragging. The mark sits in the tray all day, so it has to be calm, exact and readable at 16 px on light and dark taskbars.

orderlyprecisecalmnative

Readable at 16 px in the tray on light and dark taskbars, clearly not the Windows logo, works in one colour, says 'tiles' and 'Tessera' at once.

Final design

The gap is the T

Three window panes whose gutters spell the T of Tessera.

  • Shows exactly what the app does: a screen split into tiles
  • The name lives in the gaps, the very space Tessera puts between your windows
  • One blue pane marks the focused window, like the app's blue focus border
  • Pixel-snapped cuts for 16-64 px; light and dark tray versions follow the Windows theme
