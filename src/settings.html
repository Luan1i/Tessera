Mouse & automatic

What you can do with the mouse, and what Tessera takes care of on its own.

With the mouse

  • Drag a title bar

    Drop zones appear on the other tiles. The Swap box in the middle swaps the two windows, the Tab box near the top adds the window as a tab, and near an edge puts it on that side. Esc cancels.

  • Drag a window border

    Resizes the tile. The neighbour adjusts to fit.

  • Tab strip

    Click a tab to show it. Middle-click a tab to close it.

  • Maximize

    Goes full screen for a moment. Restore puts the window back in its tile.

Automatic

  • Blue border

    The focused window gets a blue border, so you always know where your shortcuts go.

  • Layout memory

    Tile sizes, tab groups and which app is where are saved every few seconds, and restored after a restart as your apps open.

  • Ghost windows

    Invisible or hidden windows never get a tile. If something odd still gets one, open the Window inspector () to see the program behind it and ignore it for good.

  • Pause

    Pause with . While paused, Windows gets all its own shortcuts back.

About Tessera

Tessera arranges your windows into tiles, so every window gets its own space and nothing hides behind anything else. It runs quietly in the tray, works across all your monitors, and you drive it with the keyboard or the mouse.

Open this window any time with .

How tiling works

  1. Open a window

    It gets a tile. The others make room.

  2. Close a window

    The others grow to fill the space.

  3. Rearrange

    Drag windows or use the keys to swap, resize, stack or tab them.

Reset all shortcuts?

Every action goes back to its original keys. Any shortcuts you changed will be lost.
